# Critical evaluation of the synthetic artifacts

The measured comparison establishes basic delivery and file integrity, not human realism. Perceptual listening review was unavailable in the execution environment. Claims about pronunciation, convincingness or specific audible defects would therefore be unsupported.

|---|---:|---:|
| Structure | One continuous stock voice | Nine segments with two stock voices |
| Duration | 90.115 s | 89.630 s |
| Peak amplitude relative to full scale | 0.8185 | 0.8218 |

A's advantage is structural simplicity: one voice and one generation call avoid cross-speaker assembly. Its potential weakness is that questions and answers share a voice, so listeners must infer role changes from the wording. Whether the prosody actually communicates those changes has not been heard or measured. No breathing or emotional-realism judgment is reported.

B explicitly assigns interviewer and analyst roles and permits different rate settings. That could improve navigation, but a two-speaker exchange can also imply that a real interview occurred. The opening disclosure counters that implication. Separate generation creates potential boundary artifacts and inconsistent pause lengths; those are reasons for listening review, not observed audible failures. Similar total durations do not establish equal intelligibility.

Neither file is video, so lip-sync drift, blinking, hands and visual temporal instability do not apply. The source is a fixed factual script, so the synthesis stage is not being asked to invent recommendations. A correct source script still does not prove every word was pronounced intelligibly. Decode success and absence of clipping cannot establish transcript fidelity.

No blinded audience test, real-voice comparison or validated detector was run. There is no measured answer to whether a casual listener would be fooled, or whether a specialist would notice more defects. The only “convincing enough” claim supported is that two playable, labeled speech artifacts were generated. The source-service probe failure and the loss of ordinary metadata are observed failure modes; prosodic concerns remain hypotheses.

For a final listening review, check the disclosure at the start, the pronunciation of Syracuse and both player names, the distinction between one and two additional goals, the phrase “four and a half,” the transition into every analyst answer, and preservation of the final causal limitation. These are unresolved quality checks, not claimed observations.
